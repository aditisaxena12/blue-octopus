Hey there, and welcome to my little creative corner of the internet! 👋

I'm **Aditi Saxena**, a research scholar in Computational Neuroscience who also
has experience as a software engineer. A good part of my day I stare at neurons spiking and try to
figure out what they're gossiping about. This site is where I think out loud,
share what I'm learning, and generally document the journey, detours and all.

I'm just getting started here, so bear with me while I find my feet. I promise
you'll leave with *something*, even if it's just a fun fact about brains.

When I'm not knee-deep in data, you'll find me:

- 📚 buried in a book
- 🎧 with music on way too loud
- ⚽ chasing a football around
- 🎨 painting my heart out

Am I good at any of these? Debatable. But hey, a girl's gotta have some fun,
right?

The site's pretty easy to find your way around, so go explore. One small
disclaimer before you do: what I write here is my thoughts in their rawest,
least-polished form. Please be kind, and don't take anything too personally. 💛
